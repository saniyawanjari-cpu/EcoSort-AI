\# 🌱 EcoSort AI – Identify. Decide. Dispose.



An AI-assisted waste segregation and disposal assistant designed to help people identify everyday waste and make more responsible disposal decisions.



\## 🎯 Sustainable Development Goal



\*\*Primary SDG: SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production\*\*



EcoSort AI supports responsible waste handling by helping users understand how common waste items can be segregated and disposed of appropriately.



\## 🧩 Problem Statement



People often struggle to identify the correct waste category for everyday items such as batteries, plastic containers, food waste, cardboard, and electronic devices.



Incorrect segregation can contaminate recyclable waste and make recovery and recycling more difficult.



\*\*How might we use AI to help individuals correctly identify, segregate, and dispose of everyday waste?\*\*



\## 💡 Solution



EcoSort AI provides an AI-powered interface where users can:



\- Enter a description of a waste item

\- Upload an image of waste

\- Get an AI-generated waste classification

\- Receive disposal recommendations

\- Get preparation and safety guidance

\- Understand why responsible disposal matters

\- Ask follow-up questions through the Ask EcoSort chatbot

\- See an AI confidence level for the analysis



\## 🤖 AI Components



EcoSort AI uses:



\- \*\*Multimodal AI\*\* – analyzes waste images and text descriptions

\- \*\*Generative AI\*\* – Gemini generates waste analysis and recommendations

\- \*\*Prompt Engineering\*\* – structured prompts guide the AI toward consistent waste classifications

\- \*\*Conversational AI\*\* – Ask EcoSort answers natural-language waste disposal questions

\- \*\*Structured AI Output\*\* – responses are returned in a defined format for reliable display

\- \*\*Responsible AI\*\* – uncertainty and local disposal-rule limitations are communicated to users

\## 🛡️ Responsible AI



EcoSort AI is designed with responsible AI principles in mind.



\### Transparency



The interface indicates when an analysis is generated by Gemini.



\### Uncertainty



The system provides confidence levels rather than presenting every classification as certain.



\### Privacy



Uploaded images are processed for analysis and are not intended to be permanently stored by the application.



\### Safety



Hazardous materials such as batteries receive additional safety guidance.



\### Local Rules



Waste-disposal rules vary by location. EcoSort provides general guidance and encourages users to verify local municipal requirements.
